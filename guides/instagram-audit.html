Setup, under 2 minutes

Connect Instagram to Claude

Windsor.ai streams your live reel data (views, saves, shares, watch time) into Claude.

  1. Open Connectors in Claude. In Claude or Cowork, go to Connectors, search for Windsor and add it.

Prompt 1

The audit (the roast)

Finds your top performers, tells you what's working and names the one blind spot holding you back.

The roast promptYou have full access to my Instagram account (@handle). I want you to roast me, honestly, no sugar-coating. Instagram only, no other platforms. Step 1: Pull my last 30 to 50 reels. Identify my TOP 10 performing reels (by views, saves, shares; weight saves and shares heavily, not just likes). Step 2: For each of those 10 reels, analyze: - Hook type - Topic / content pillar - Format (talking head, b-roll, text-on-screen, etc.) - What's WORKING (be specific: the hook? the pacing? the topic?) - What's NOT working / what's holding it back from going bigger Step 3: Output the analysis as a clean, visually striking PDF I can save and screen-record. Requirements for the visual: - A big bold table or card layout, one row/card per reel - Columns: Reel #, Views, Hook, What's Working (green), What's Not (red) - Use color (green = working, red = not), large readable type, generous spacing; it needs to look GOOD on a phone screen when I film it - Header at the top: "Claude's Instagram Audit, @handle" - At the bottom: a 3-line "biggest blind spot" verdict
Prompt 2

The reel scriptwriter

Turns the audit into 3 ready-to-film reel scripts in your own voice, matched to a proven structure.

Scriptwriter promptUse the reel-scriptwriter skill to write me 3 Instagram Reel scripts (30 to 55 seconds each) in MY authentic voice, based on the strategy you just built. CRITICAL: match the structure of this reel I just shot: [HOOK: 1 punchy sentence that creates intrigue or stakes] [CONTEXT: 1 to 2 sentences that raise the stakes or set up credibility, e.g. "I have 455K followers and..."] [PROMISE: "Here are the 3 things..." or equivalent] [SERIES TEASE: "This is part X of [series name]"] #1) [Point one: include a personal/vulnerable beat, not just info] #2) [Point two: include a tangible outcome or artifact] #3) [Point three: include a meta moment that ties back to THIS reel, e.g. "the reel you're watching right now is the result"] [CTA: comment-bait with a specific keyword, e.g. "Comment X and I'll send you..."] [FOLLOW CTA: "Follow for more..."] For each script, also give me: - 3 hook variants so I can pick the strongest - On-screen text cues - Caption (in my voice, with the same comment-bait CTA) Pull tone, phrasing, and rhythm from my last 20 captions and reels. My audience: creators, AI-curious professionals, and beginners. I'm known for making AI feel approachable. At least one of the three scripts should be PERSONAL, something I've been avoiding posting.

Bonus prompts

Three prompts I keep on rotation

Run them after the audit.

The 7-day content calendar

Based on the audit you just did, build me a 7-day Instagram content calendar. For each day give me: the format (reel/carousel/story), the content pillar, a one-line hook, and why it fits my strategy. Lead with my strengths, fix my blind spot. Keep it realistic for someone posting once a day, not five times a day.

The hook doctor

Here is a hook I wrote: [PASTE YOUR FLAT HOOK]. Rewrite it 5 ways using proven viral hook formulas (curiosity gap, bold claim, direct question, number + confession, "nobody is talking about this"). Keep each under 12 words. Tell me which one you'd bet on and why.

Captions in my voice

Write 3 caption options for this reel: [DESCRIBE THE REEL]. Match the tone, rhythm, and phrasing of my last 20 captions. Keep it short. Start with a comment-bait CTA using the keyword [KEYWORD].
Go further

Find the reels going viral in your niche

The audit shows what works on your profile. This shows what's working in your niche right now. Connect Apify so Claude can pull real reels and transcribe them.

  1. Add the Apify connector in Claude. Go to Connectors, search for Apify and add it. You need a free Apify account.
  2. Pick 3 to 5 creators in your niche. Accounts slightly bigger than you, whose reels keep showing up in your feed.
  3. Paste the research prompt. Claude pulls their recent reels, transcribes the top performers and breaks down why they work.
Research promptUsing the Apify connector, pull the most recent reels from these creators in my niche: [@creator1, @creator2, @creator3]. Find the 5 reels with the highest views relative to each creator's follower count. Transcribe them, and for each one break down: - The hook (first 3 seconds, word for word) - The structure (how it holds attention to the end) - Why it's working right now (topic, format, or delivery) Then tell me which ONE format I should recreate first, and why it fits MY account (use the audit you did on my profile earlier in this chat).

Schedule this reel to Instagram via Metricool. Caption: [paste your caption] First check my best times to post this week and pick the strongest slot. Show me what you scheduled before it goes live.
