Put yourself into any viral video

This is exactly how I put myself into that fancy cafe video. I didn't film anything. I took the original clip, made an AI character of myself and let the AI swap the people, while every move, the timing and the room stayed the same.

Before you start

What you need

A short clip4 to 30 seconds that you have the right to use, one or two people and a still camera.
A few photos of youFront, side and full body, to build your character sheet.
GPT Image 2.5Or any image tool you like, for the character sheet and the second person.
HiggsfieldI used Genjutsu with Objects swap.
Step 1

Make a character sheet of yourself

This is the step that made the biggest difference for me. With a single photo, the AI tends to make me look older and more wrinkled than I am. So I first make a character sheet in GPT Image 2.5 from real photos of myself: front, side, full body and a few expressions.

It still looks like me, just cleaner, and the video model gets every angle it needs.

Upload 4 to 6 stills of yourself (frames from a video you filmed work great, ideally from different angles) and send this:

Character sheet promptCreate a character sheet of me from these stills. They're frames from a video I filmed, so this is me. Make it look as much like me as possible: same face, eyes, nose, smile, hair and features. Keep my real age. Don't make me look older and don't add extra wrinkles. I want to look good, but still like myself. Remove anything I'm holding, like a microphone. Include: one large close-up portrait, my head from the front, three-quarter and side, a few different expressions, my full body from the front, side and back, and close-ups of my eye, skin, lips and ear. Same outfit and the same soft lighting in every image.
Step 2

Create the second person

If there's someone else in the clip, don't use a real stranger. Create an invented person instead. Ask for a candid phone photo, not a model shot, so they look like a real person.

Give them a look and colors that are clearly different from yours, so the AI doesn't mix your faces.

Step 3

Open Genjutsu in Higgsfield

Go to Video, open Genjutsu and choose Objects swap, not Motion transfer.

  • Upload your clip under Add a reference video
  • Upload your character sheet first, then the second person
  • Turn on Prompt and paste the prompt below
Step 4

Paste the prompt

Fill in the [brackets] with your own details. In Higgsfield, type @ and pick Video 1, Image 1 and Image 2 from the list, so the tags link to your uploads. If that doesn't work, write "the video", "image 1" and "image 2" instead.

The swap promptEdit @Video1. Keep every movement, gesture, head turn, step, position and all timing exactly as in the video, frame for frame, with the same camera and the same room. Only the people change. Keep any on-screen text exactly as it is. The [person you replace] becomes a [woman/man] whose face and hair match @Image1 100%: same face, [your hair]. She does exactly what he does, in the same places and at the same moments: [short summary of the action]. Her body is [your real build, e.g. athletic and feminine: toned arms, narrow shoulders, slim waist, strong legs]. She sits with a straight, upright back, chest open and shoulders relaxed and down, never hunched or rounded. Her eyes look where his eyes look, [where they look], never into the camera. [Expression]. She wears [your outfit], with exactly two legs and two feet. The [second person in the video] becomes the person in @Image2 100%: same face, [their hair and outfit]. She does exactly what the [second person] in the video does. The two people look clearly different from each other. Photoreal, real skin texture that matches @Image1 exactly with no added wrinkles or ageing, natural fabric movement, same light as the video, real-time speed. Exactly two people. No new text, no logos, no watermark.
Step 5

Test, then finish

Run one test first and check it closely before you spend more credits. Then add your own text and music on top in your favorite editor.

What I learned

My tips

1
Don't describe every move. The AI can already see the video. A short summary of the action works better.
2
Write your real body. If you don't, you end up with the original person's body. Posture matters too: I had to add "upright back, never hunched".
3
Different colors for different people. Two people in the same color look too alike.
4
Watch the clothes. Long coats and blazers tend to glide through furniture, and that's what gives the AI away.
5
Make it yours. Change the outfits, the people and the text, and credit the original creator of the clip.

The swap prompt is built on Cindy Zhu's (@cindiezhu) template, with my own changes.

Have fun! If you make one, tag me @shedoesai ♡
