Get Arcads

Inspiration: Jean Phil (@jean_philanthrope) blew up in just a few days as an AI character.

Step 1

Create your character

  1. Open Arcads and go to the Image tab.
  2. Choose GPT Image 2, set the format to 9:16 and turn prompt enhancer off.
  3. Paste the prompt below and hit generate. Make a few versions and pick your favourite.

This is the prompt for the character I made in the reel. Change the hair, face or outfit to make it your own.

Character promptHyper-realistic editorial portrait photo of a quirky young man in his mid 20s. Thick glossy copper-auburn hair cut into a perfectly blunt bowl bob with heavy straight bangs, the ends flaring out wide like a bell. A long, elegant curled handlebar mustache in the same copper tone. Pale skin with natural freckles and visible pores, very long slender neck, slightly droopy half-closed eyes, an unimpressed deadpan expression, lips slightly pouted. He wears a plain cream crew-neck t-shirt. Framing: chest-up, head and shoulders fully visible, centered, facing the camera. Background: flat dusty-blue studio backdrop. Lighting: soft even studio light, shot on a medium format camera, sharp focus. No filters, no retouching, no text. Vertical 9:16.
💡 Tip: Keep the head and upper body clearly visible with nothing covering them. Replace Actor needs that to work well.
Step 2

Find your trending video

Save a trending video where one person is on camera. Clear lighting and a person facing the camera give the best result.

💡 Tip: The person should take up a good part of the frame, head and upper body visible. Tiny people far away in the background won't work.
Step 3

Put your character into the video

  1. Go to Arcads and click See more.
  2. Under Tools, find Replace Actor.
  1. Upload your character from Step 1.
  2. Upload the trending video.
  3. Hit generate. After a few minutes, Arcads recreates the video with your character doing the exact same moves.
🔁 Not happy? Generate again, or try a new version of your character from Step 1.
Make it your own

My tips

1
One signature detail. Give your character something people remember: a hairstyle, a mustache, a colour.
2
Same character, every video. Use it across every post, so people start recognising it.
3
Only use clips you're allowed to use. And label your post as AI-generated wherever the platform asks.
