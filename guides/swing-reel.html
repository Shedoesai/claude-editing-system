Make a swing reel with Claude

This is the skill I used to make that reel: a swinging ball that knocks the text in, a spotlight, the "how it works" steps and sound on every move. No camera, no editing app, no After Effects. You just tell Claude what your reel should say.

Before you start

What you need

A paid Claude planWith Cowork, it's in the Claude app.
The skill fileswing-reel.zip, download it below.
Your logosOptional: PNG or SVG if you want a logo above a line.
Step 1

Add the skill to Claude

Download swing-reel.zip. In the Claude app, open Settings, find Skills and upload the zip. Don't unzip it, Claude reads it as it is.

swing-reel.zipThe free swing reel skill
Download
Step 2

Start a chat in Cowork

Open Cowork and start a new chat, or a project called "My reels", so every reel lives in one place. Then send:

Use the swing reel skill to make me a reel. Set up everything you need yourself.

Claude installs the tools it needs. You don't touch any code.

Step 3

Tell Claude what your reel should say

Give Claude your lines, short and punchy. This is exactly what I used:

Lines: "Claude made this reel." with the Claude logo and a green check. "Without After Effects." with the After Effects icon and a red cross. "Just one skill." Spotlight: "You can steal it." Steps: Install the skill, Describe your idea, Render the reel. My handle is @yourname and the comment keyword is YOURWORD.

Upload your logos in the same message. No idea what to write? Just tell Claude your topic and ask it to write the lines for you.

Step 4

Check the preview

Claude sends you a 1080x1920 preview with sound. Say what to change, one thing at a time, like "move how it works a bit lower" or "make the follow text bigger". Ask for a new numbered version each time (v1, v2, v3).

Step 5

Get the final

When you love it, say:

Looks great, send me the final in 4K.

Download, post, done.

What I learned

My tips

1
Keep every line short. Two to four words per row looks best.
2
One color does the talking. Pick one accent color and use it for the words that matter.
3
Make it yours. Change the colors, the steps and the end card, so it fits your brand.

Have fun! If you make one, tag me @shedoesai ♡
