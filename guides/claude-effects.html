part 1

Edit your reels with Claude

I just chat with Claude in Cowork, like I would with an editor. Here's how.

Step 1

Start a project for your reels

Open Claude, go to Cowork and create a project, for example "My reels". Give each new video its own chat inside the project, so everything stays in one place.

Step 2

Let Claude set up Hyperframes

Hyperframes is the free, open source tool Claude uses to build and render the video. You don't install anything yourself. Just paste this into the chat:

Please set up Hyperframes (github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) and use it to edit my reels.

Claude takes care of the rest.

Step 3

Upload your video and script

Drop your raw clip into the chat and paste your script. Then send something like this:

Edit this into a vertical 9:16 reel. Transcribe it and compare it to my script. Cut out the pauses and bad takes, but keep my reactions. Add small captions written from the script and timed to my voice. Keep all text inside the TikTok and Instagram safe zone. Send me a 1080x1920 version to check.

Claude cuts, adds captions and sends the video back to you in the chat.

Step 4

Add effects

Now the fun part. Tell Claude which effect you want on which word, for example:

When I say "whoa", put text behind me. When I say "camera shake", shake the frame. Add a zoom at every jump cut. Use one accent color: orange.

The more specific you are about the word and the moment, the better it lands. Want ready-made effects? That's Part 2.

Step 5

Give feedback, then get the final

Watch it and just say what to change, like "captions a bit smaller" or "move the logo earlier". Ask for a new numbered version each time (v1, v2, v3) so you can always go back.

When you love it, say:

Looks great, send me the final in 4K.

Download, post, done.

My editing tips

My editing tips

1
Your script is the truth. Ask Claude to write captions from your script, not from what it heard. It sometimes mishears words.
2
One change at a time. Small rounds of feedback get better results than one long list.
3
Save what works. When you find a style you like, ask Claude to remember it, so your next reel starts there.
Bonus: edit in any creator's style. Want your reels to look like Hormozi, Ali Abdaal or Dan Koe? Claude can do that too. The real magic is mixing a few styles into one that's only yours.

What I learned

My effect tips

1
One effect per moment. Let each effect land for 1 to 2 seconds before the next one.
2
Put the best one first. Your strongest effect is your hook.
3
Keep it in the safe zone. Ask Claude to keep all text and cards away from the edges, so the app buttons don't cover them.
4
Use your own colors. Tell Claude one accent color, and every effect will match.
